A live news-conditioned prediction pipeline, plus an event study over 86,170 posts (2009–2026) asking a question the modelling usually skips: how much of this is predictable from news at all?

Four models benchmarked: Markov, trigram, LoRA fine-tuned Llama 3 8B, and RAG. They tie within noise. Read against the 10–16% ceiling, that is closer to the task limit than it first appears.
Two traps this corrects for. One post opens correlated positions across SPY/QQQ/DIA/IWM, so counting each as an independent observation inflated n from 38 → 99 and moved p from 0.053 → 0.0105 — every result here is clustered by event. And a single coin-flip is not a control at small n, so the null is averaged over 2,000 random-direction draws.

How this page gets its data. News and the Kalshi market are fetched live on every request. Posts cannot be: Truth Social sits behind Cloudflare and returns 403 to datacenter IPs, so a serverless function can never reach it. They arrive instead from a collector running on a residential IP, which also keeps a single owner of the rate limit. Serverless functions are stateless, so this page accumulates no history of its own. The prior above comes from Kalshi's own settled weeks, without the partial-week conditioning that is the actual edge — that needs continuous collection, which runs separately. One number worth knowing: the weekly posting rate roughly doubled after February 2026 (~98 → ~198), so a model fitted on the corpus alone would confidently bet the wrong brackets.
